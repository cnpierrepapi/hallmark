This page generates real images, stamps proof into them, and then lets you break that proof yourself. Six steps, nothing pre-recorded unless the daily budget runs out.

1
Generate

Say what you want.

Seven images, one brief, submitted together. Same instruction every time, and they will not all come back equally good. That is the whole reason a gate exists.

2
Pick

You choose. The record keeps the rest.

Click the one you want. Say why, in your own words. No model here can see an image, so nothing pretends to judge your taste: your reason plus the measured checks are what the notes are written from.

Everything you did not pick is stored too, with a note on why it lost.

The chosen image now carries a hash and a reference struck into the file. Nothing is taken on trust: the checker re-hashes the bytes you would download.

Inventory

The rejects are the interesting part.

Every attempt is kept in storage, not just the winner. Acceptance rates, latency and what a usable asset really costs are unknowable if you bin the failures. This inventory stays with this browser.
