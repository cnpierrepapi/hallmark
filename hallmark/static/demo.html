The style is a preset, so the full prompt is ours and stays withheld. Your brief is your own words and is shown back to you.

2
Pick

You choose. Say why in your own words.

Click the one you want and write the reason you picked it. No model here can see an image, so nothing pretends to judge your taste. Your reason is what the rejection notes are written from: say the stance was better and the notes answer on stance, not on file sizes.

Your name goes into the record and into the file, so the approval has a person on it. Everything you did not pick is stored too, with the note on why it lost.

6
Inventory

The rejects are the interesting part.

Every attempt is kept in storage, not just the winner. Acceptance rates, latency and what a usable asset really costs are unknowable if you bin the failures. Note that only the chosen one is signed: download a reject and its properties are bare, because nobody approved it.
