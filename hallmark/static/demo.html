2
Pick

You choose. Say why in your own words.

Click the one you want and write the reason you picked it. No model here can see a picture, so nothing pretends to judge your taste. Your reason is what the rejection notes are written from: say the stance was better and the notes answer on stance, not on file sizes.

Your name goes into the record and into the file, so the approval has a person on it. Everything you did not pick is stored too, with the note on why it lost.

4
Edit

Now change it.

Two kinds of edit, and the record refuses both. The record itself stays valid the whole time. It simply stops describing this file.

An edit you can see

The sort of thing that happens between approval and publication, every day.

An edit you cannot

One byte, deep in the image data. Your eye will not catch it. The hash will.

Take the credit out

What a platform does to your file on upload. Not one frame changes, and it still fails, because the credit was inside the bytes that were hashed.

An edit you cannot see

One byte, deep in the video data. Nobody watching it would notice.

A clip off this pipeline is well past the size this platform will accept in a request, so it never leaves storage. The edit is made on a throwaway copy that is deleted before the answer comes back. Nothing you generated is touched.

6
Inventory

The rejects are the interesting part.

Every attempt from this run is kept in storage, not just the winner. Acceptance rates, latency and what a usable asset really costs are unknowable if you bin the failures. Note that only the chosen one is signed: download a reject and its properties are bare, because nobody approved it.

Record

From 2 August 2026 an output like this has to be marked in a machine readable way, and someone eventually has to show that it was.
